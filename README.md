# ARENA · ARN · UD0

**William R. Forstchen's *Arena* (1994)** — the **first Magic: The Gathering novel ever
published** — catalogued into **UD0** as a book-world and the literary companion to the
[MTG Arena](https://davidwise01.github.io/mtg-arena/) game-sphere.

A colorless, one-eyed mage named **Garth One-Eye** walks into the city of Estark at Festival,
turns its four dueling Houses against each other in the Arena, and avenges a fifth House
destroyed a generation ago — defeating the ascended planeswalker Kuthuman. *Magic's first myth
was a Western with mana.*

Standing template: The Arc · The Book · **How It Renders Magic** (mana, summoning, and the
mortal **ante** — early Magic's now-removed play-for-keeps mechanic) · **Real or Fluff**
(foundational pulp; pre-lore canon; and the loveliest true fact — **Garth One-Eye became a real
WUBRG card in 2021** that conjures the Black Lotus) · The Message. 12 emergents, full `.dlw` each.
